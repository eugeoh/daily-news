# Daily AI Lab Digest — 2026-10-06

Grab your second cup—this week is making it painfully obvious that the era of the pure, ad-free AI sandbox is officially wrapping up. Between OpenAI blowing the doors open on ChatGPT ads to quietly baking invisible watermark tags into its text for European regulators, the business model of generative AI is growing up fast. At the same time, practical upgrades like GitHub’s new code-review benchmark prove the tech is getting sharper at the gritty day-to-day work, meaning the tools we rely on are finally getting both smarter and a whole lot louder.

## Frontier Model & Research

### [Our approach to EU text provenance rules](https://openai.com/index/eu-text-provenance)
_OpenAI · 2026-10-05_

OpenAI is explaining how it plans to follow European Union rules by adding invisible digital tags to text generated by its artificial intelligence. This matters because it creates a standard way to spot AI-written content, which helps people know when they are reading something created by a machine rather than a human.


## Software Engineering

### [ReviewBench: An open benchmark for AI code review](https://github.blog/ai-and-ml/github-copilot/reviewbench-an-open-benchmark-for-ai-code-review/)
_GitHub Blog · 2026-10-05_

GitHub has released a new testing system called ReviewBench to measure how well artificial intelligence tools can review computer code. This matters because it gives developers a reliable way to compare different AI coding assistants and figure out which ones actually save time and catch mistakes.


## Enterprise

### [Building advertising for the way people use AI](https://openai.com/index/new-chatgpt-ads-format-and-measurement)
_OpenAI · 2026-10-05_

OpenAI is introducing visual ads directly inside ChatGPT along with new tools for advertisers to track their performance. This marks a major shift for the company as it begins turning its massive free user base into a traditional advertising business.
