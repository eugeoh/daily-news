**What matters today**
GitBook launched automated documentation audit tools and native MCP integration, enabling AI agents to programmatically analyze and maintain knowledge bases. For Mintlify, this highlights a massive opening: as downstream coding agents and enterprise workflows increasingly demand pristine, programmatic knowledge sources, the winners will be the platforms that seamlessly bridge documentation health with autonomous agent execution.
