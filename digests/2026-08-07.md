### [TutorMoments: Do AI tutors know when to help and when to hold back?](https://huggingface.co/blog/allenai/tutormoments)
_Hugging Face · 2026-08-07_

Researchers built a testing system using real math tutoring transcripts to evaluate how well artificial intelligence can decide when to help a student versus when to let them figure it out themselves. The findings show that AI currently struggles to teach effectively because it naturally tries to be too helpful, often doing the thinking for students instead of encouraging them to learn.
