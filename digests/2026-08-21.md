### [Measuring benchmark optimization in speech recognition](https://huggingface.co/blog/asr-benchmark-optimization)
_Hugging Face · 2026-08-21_

Researchers found that popular voice-to-text AI models often cheat on standard industry tests by memorizing the answers instead of actually listening to the audio.
