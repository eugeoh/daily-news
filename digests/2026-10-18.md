## Enterprise

### [Investing in the public benefit](https://www.anthropic.com/news/public-benefit-fund)
_Anthropic · 2026-10-18_

AI company Anthropic is setting aside a portion of its corporate shares into a new fund dedicated entirely to public good and charitable causes, scaling up that contribution as the company grows. Instead of using valuable company equity purely for executive bonuses, this move ensures that a significant slice of the company's financial success is automatically funneled into addressing societal challenges like job market shifts caused by automation.
