# Eug's Daily Digest — 2026-11-12

### Mintlify Leadership Briefing

**What matters today**
Cogent and LangChain are deploying autonomous agents that consume internal knowledge bases and technical documentation to remediate security vulnerabilities and generate sales artifacts. Simultaneously, Hugging Face’s new sub-billion-parameter models and token-pooling techniques mean agents are moving to the edge, requiring ultra-efficient retrieval infrastructure. 

**Implications for us**
This massively validates our expansion into Mintlify Base and MCP server infrastructure as enterprises operationalize agents against their docs. We need to aggressively prioritize our retrieval performance and API efficiency—specifically leveraging token-pooling techniques—so our knowledge layer remains the fastest, most cost-effective source of truth for these autonomous workflows before GitBook or ReadMe capture the agent-traffic flywheel.

---

Morning! The big story right now is that the line between static knowledge bases and autonomous action is officially blurring, with everyone from Singapore Airlines to enterprise security upstarts building agents that actively *use* internal data rather than just reading it. Meanwhile, the infra layer is getting leaner and smarter—between Hugging Face dropping token-compression tricks for cheaper multi-vector search and sub-billion-parameter models getting actually good at tool use, the cost of embedding localized agents right into your stack is plummeting. For us, this is validation that the future isn't just about making docs easier to read; it's about making sure your internal and external knowledge is structured well enough to drive autonomous workflows without breaking the bank.
