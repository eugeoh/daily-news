**Implications for us**
This validates our thesis that agent traffic is dominating our platform and shifts the battleground directly to agent observability and knowledge retrieval. Morning! The big story right now is how the ground is shifting beneath "knowledge discovery" as agents move from answering simple search queries to executing entire operational workflows—from automated website research in sales to cross-repo engineering maintenance at scale. At the same time, infrastructure is maturing fast to handle the mess, with new tracing layers like LangChain's agent lake showing that the bottleneck isn't getting agents to run, it's capturing and trusting their execution data. For us at Mintlify, this is a massive green light: as internal systems and external knowledge bases become the primary operating system for these autonomous agents, the winners will be the platforms that don't just host docs, but make all that organizational context readable, structured, and bulletproof for AI to act on.
