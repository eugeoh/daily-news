### [How to build a knowledge factory for AI agents](https://www.langchain.com/blog/knowledge-factory)
_LangChain · 2026-11-21_

LangChain introduced the knowledge factory, a self-updating system where agents flag missing company data and a curator agent automatically fixes it. This transforms documentation from static reference material into a dynamic feedback loop, allowing autonomous agents to continuously maintain their own knowledge base without manual developer intervention.

### [Securely connect Replit to your private network with Tunnels](https://replit.com/blog/build-with-private-data-using-tunnels)
_Replit · 2026-11-20_

Replit has launched Tunnels to securely connect cloud-based development environments and AI agents to services behind private corporate networks using mTLS and granular access controls. This development removes a major security barrier for autonomous AI agents, enabling them to safely query internal databases and knowledge sources without compromising network security.
